# The Data Dialogues

Playlist Link: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLnRfQfvUKgKJX72jCOHG4d1CQWnqA_GM_

## Over hundreds of views and counting!

The Data Dialogues are a series of conversations with members of the DataJam community. Participants include students, teachers, professors, working professionals, and everything in between! Data comes in all different forms, and so do its practictioners: these conversations include stories and insights from paramedics, engineers, and rocket scientists, just to name a few!

These episodes highlight our community members' stories, projects, and journies throughout data. They could be using it full time or just starting out. The goal of this series is to provide an approachable series of conversations for those interested in learning more about data and the DataJam. By demystifying the buzzwords and hearing from the humans at the heart of these amazing projects, we aim to show that data is for everyone. Happy listening!
